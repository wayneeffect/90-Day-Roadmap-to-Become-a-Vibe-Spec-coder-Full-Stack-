**Vibe/Spec coder (Full-Stack) salaries in 2026 typically fall in the broader “vibe coding” / AI-assisted full-stack developer range.** Exact figures vary by experience, location, company type, equity, and whether the role is full-time employee vs. contractor. Pure “Vibe/Spec coder” titles are still emerging, so data comes mainly from vibe coding / AI-native full-stack job listings and trackers.

### Typical U.S. / Remote (USD annual base salary ranges)
Data aggregated from live job trackers (GoodVibeCode, Remote Vibe Coding Jobs, and related 2026 reports):

| Experience Level       | Typical Range          | Notes / Averages |
|------------------------|------------------------|------------------|
| Entry / Junior (0–2 yrs) | $70k – $110k          | Avg listed ~$85k–$109k; strong portfolios or startups can reach ~$120k |
| Mid-level (2–5 yrs)    | $100k – $180k         | Common band $120k–$150k; AI tools fluency adds premium |
| Senior (5–10 yrs)      | $140k – $250k+        | Avg listed ~$140k–$194k; full-stack AI-native often $170k–$250k |
| Staff / Principal (10+) | $160k – $350k+       | Higher end at well-funded startups or big tech with equity |

- **Overall median** across tracked vibe coding roles: around **$135k–$160k**.
- AI-forward companies often pay a **15–25% premium** over traditional full-stack roles (traditional U.S. full-stack average is roughly $136k).

### Other Compensation Notes
- **Equity, bonuses, and benefits** are common at startups and can significantly increase total compensation (especially at Series B+ companies).
- **Contract / freelance / day rates**: Strong portfolios command $100–$200+/hour or $400–$1,200/day. Some global remote contractor roles list lower fixed amounts (e.g., ~$60k/year).
- **Specific examples**: Entry-ish remote roles $45k–$75k; European AI-native full-stack ~$80k–$110k; some senior full-stack AI roles higher.

### Location Differences
- **U.S. coastal / high-cost areas or remote-US**: Toward the higher end of the ranges above.
- **Global remote / lower-cost countries**: Often lower (e.g., India typical bands roughly ₹4–9 LPA junior to ₹25–55 LPA staff/expert, or ~$5k–$65k USD equivalent).
- Remote roles are very common (~46% in some trackers).

### Key Factors That Affect Pay
- Proven ability to ship full-stack products end-to-end with AI tools (Cursor, Claude Code, Lovable, Bolt, etc.) plus solid fundamentals.
- Experience combining “vibe” speed with “spec”-driven reliability.
- Stack (e.g., Next.js/React/TypeScript + modern backend is in demand).
- Company stage (startups may offer more equity; established firms more base + benefits).

Salaries are still evolving as the role matures. For the most current figures, check live trackers such as GoodVibeCode or Remote Vibe Coding Jobs, Levels.fyi, or recent postings, and factor in total compensation rather than base alone.
