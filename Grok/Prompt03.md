**Formal education requirements for a Vibe/Spec coder (Full-Stack) are flexible and often secondary to skills, experience, and a strong portfolio.** This emerging AI-assisted role prioritizes the ability to ship full-stack applications quickly using tools like Cursor, Claude Code, Lovable, or Bolt, combined with solid engineering judgment (especially for the “spec” side of structured planning).

### Typical Requirements from Job Postings
- **Preferred or listed**: A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field. Some postings (especially at larger or more traditional firms) explicitly prefer this.
- **Common alternative**: “Or equivalent practical experience.” Many listings accept this, recognizing self-taught developers, bootcamp graduates, or those with proven project experience.
- **Not always required**: Senior or startup-focused roles (e.g., some Sr. Vibe-Coder postings) often omit a degree requirement entirely and focus on years of professional full-stack experience (commonly 3–5+ years), tool fluency, and a track record of shipping.

Examples from 2025–2026 postings include phrases like “Bachelor’s degree… or equivalent practical experience,” while others emphasize hands-on AI tool use, end-to-end ownership of features, and fundamentals (data structures, APIs, databases, Git, etc.) without mentioning degrees.

### Industry Perspective
Leaders in the vibe-coding space, such as the CEO of Lovable (a major AI app-building platform), have publicly stated that a traditional CS degree is **not necessary**. The value of formal education has shifted because AI tools allow people to turn ideas into working products without years of traditional training. What matters more is:
- Curiosity and fast learning/adaptability
- Ability to ship high-quality products quickly
- Strong judgment to evaluate and refine AI-generated code (spotting bugs, security issues, architectural problems)
- Clear communication and product sense

### Practical Paths That Work
- **Self-taught or bootcamp** backgrounds are viable, especially with a portfolio of deployed full-stack projects built (or heavily accelerated) with AI tools.
- Certificates or short courses focused on AI-assisted/vibe coding exist and can help demonstrate skills.
- Prior experience in frontend, backend, full-stack, design engineering, or product roles provides a strong advantage, as experienced developers often excel at guiding AI effectively.
- For pure beginners: Learning to *read* and critically evaluate code is essential even if AI writes most of it; pure “prompt-only” without fundamentals has limits for production roles.

**Bottom line**: A CS or related bachelor’s degree helps and is preferred by some employers, but it is rarely a hard barrier. Demonstrable skills—shipping real full-stack apps end-to-end with AI tools, solid fundamentals, and the ability to combine rapid “vibe” iteration with structured “spec”-driven reliability—carry far more weight in 2026 hiring for this role. A public portfolio (GitHub, live demos) is typically the strongest signal.
